Controls

↑ ↓ drive forward / reverse (hold)
← → turn the wheel (it stays where you leave it)
Shift hold for fine control (25%)
C center wheel · R reset car · F fit views
V cycle views: both, top only, driver only
Q E rotate car in place (placement only, not physical)
Top view: drag the car to place it, drag elsewhere to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
Driver view: scroll or pinch to zoom. The blue "G" compass points towards the garage wall.

Model notes

Kinematic bicycle model around the rear axle, no tire slip, walking speed. Max wheel angle is derived from the curb to curb turning circle and track width. Height (ceiling, ramps) is ignored. "Door can close" means the whole car, mirrors included, is behind the inner face of the door wall.
