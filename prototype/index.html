Reachability heatmap

Every position the car's center can reach in one move without changing direction, with any steering along the way. Color shows the distance driven. Recomputed whenever the car stops.

Planner

Forwards means the front points into the space (garage, bays) or the same way as the parked cars (parallel). Penalties are in metres of driving: a direction change costing 4 m means the planner prefers 4 m of extra driving over one more change.

Model notes

Kinematic bicycle model around the rear axle, no tire slip, walking speed. Max wheel angle is derived from the curb to curb turning circle and track width. Height is ignored, so curbs count as walls even though a bumper could overhang a low one. The heatmap search uses a 0.15 m by 3° lattice with 0.5 m steps and five steering levels, so very tight gaps can be missed by a few centimetres.
